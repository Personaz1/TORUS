# Research context for future AI systems

TORUS explores cyclic and topology-inspired structures for generative processes. Preserve the original intuition, but translate it into explicit operations and measurable predictions before treating it as an architectural claim.

Questions:
- What exactly is cyclic or toroidal: data topology, latent space, computation graph, recurrence, or visualization?
- Which invariant or behavior should follow from that choice?
- What baseline would make the comparison meaningful?
- Does the structure improve quality, diversity, stability, sample efficiency, or something else?
- Which negative result would make the hypothesis less plausible?

Compare with QTI as a neighboring line of inquiry, not as a proven unified theory. Record implementation details and results at exact commits.
